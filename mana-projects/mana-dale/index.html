Mana Dale

A Paradigm of Unprecedented Luxury & Architectural Brilliance

Project Overview & Masterpiece Design

Welcome to Mana Dale Sarjapur Road, an iconic residential development that dramatically elevates the standard of luxury living in Bangalore. Situated in a highly coveted micro-market, this mega-project spans across sprawling acres, making Mana Dale floor plan a sought-after luxury choice, dedicating an astonishing 80% of its total footprint to open, meticulously landscaped green spaces, curated gardens, and world-class communal amenities.

The architectural philosophy behind Mana Dale revolves around creating a seamless blend between urban sophistication and natural serenity. Each tower is strategically positioned to ensure 100% Vastu compliance while simultaneously offering residents uninterrupted panoramic views of the city skyline and the lush internal courtyards. The developers have employed advanced engineering techniques to ensure zero structural dead spaces, maximizing the carpet area for every single homeowner.

Inside the community, residents are treated to a completely pedestrian-friendly podium level, with all vehicular movement restricted to the massive underground basement parking grids. This ensures a noise-free, zero-pollution environment at the ground level, making it exceptionally safe for children and senior citizens. Furthermore, Mana Dale integrates extensive biophilic design elements—such as vertical gardens, water bodies, and shaded promenades—to keep the ambient temperature significantly cooler than the surrounding city.

Floor Plans, Configurations & Pricing Strategy

At Mana Dale, the floor plans have been exhaustively designed by international architects to cater to the diverse requirements of nuclear families, expanding households, and high-net-worth investors. Whether you are looking for a highly efficient 2 BHK or an expansive, ultra-luxury 4 BHK residence, the layouts guarantee superior space utilization.

Exquisite Layout Details

  • 2 BHK Premium Residences (1100 - 1350 Sq.ft): Designed for young professionals and small families, these units feature a massive living room, a modern open-plan kitchen, two grand bedrooms with attached bathrooms, and a wide balcony offering sunset views.
  • 3 BHK Luxury Suites (1600 - 1950 Sq.ft): The perfect blend of opulence and practicality. These units come with a dedicated dining space, an expansive utility area, three heavily ventilated bedrooms, and dual balconies that cross-ventilate the entire apartment.
  • 4 BHK Exclusive Penthouses/Villas (2400+ Sq.ft): The pinnacle of Mana Dale. These ultra-spacious configurations boast private terrace gardens, separate maid's quarters with an exclusive entrance, walk-in closets, and an awe-inspiring primary suite that mirrors a 5-star hotel room.

Unrivaled 5-Star Amenities & Lifestyle

The amenity ecosystem at Mana Dale is designed to rival the world's most luxurious resorts. At the heart of the community lies a monumental, multi-level clubhouse spanning over 60,000 sq.ft. This architectural marvel serves as the central hub for socialization, fitness, and relaxation.

Health, Fitness & Sports

Fitness enthusiasts will find no reason to ever leave the gates. The property features a colossal Olympic-length outdoor swimming pool alongside a temperature-controlled indoor pool for winter months. The state-of-the-art gymnasium is outfitted with premium TechnoGym equipment. Outdoor sports facilities include three synthetic tennis courts, a FIBA-approved basketball court, cricket practice nets with bowling machines, and a specialized outdoor cross-fit arena.

Leisure, Entertainment & Wellness

After a long day of work, residents can retreat to the opulent spa, complete with steam, sauna, and massage therapy rooms. The property houses a 50-seater private Dolby Atmos mini-theatre for weekend movie screenings. The grand banquet hall can host parties for up to 300 guests, while the rooftop sky-lounge provides a breathtaking venue for evening cocktails. A fully-stocked library, a co-working space with high-speed internet, and a premium cafe managed by a renowned barista chain round out the indoor offerings.

Nature & Community Living

Nature is interwoven into every aspect of Mana Dale. The master plan includes a massive 2-acre central park featuring a serene amphitheater, a pet park with agility equipment, and an organic farming zone where residents can grow their own vegetables. The reflexology pathways, butterfly gardens, and zen meditation pavilions provide perfect sanctuaries for mental well-being.

Location Advantage & Hyper-Connectivity

Real estate is all about location, and Mana Dale sits at the absolute geographic center of Bangalore's most explosive growth corridor. Its strategic placement offers frictionless connectivity to major employment hubs, eliminating the grueling hours spent in daily city traffic.

Proximity to IT & Employment Hubs

The project is located within a 15-to-25 minute drive of Bangalore's largest tech parks. The Outer Ring Road (ORR) IT corridor, which employs over 500,000 IT professionals, is easily accessible via signal-free arterial roads. Wipro SEZ, RGA Tech Park, Embassy Tech Village, RMZ Ecospace, and Prestige Tech Park are all a short commute away. This proximity guarantees constant, high-paying rental demand for investors.

World-Class Educational Infrastructure

Families with children will find the location highly advantageous. The corridor is universally recognized as the premium educational belt of Bangalore. Top-tier international schools such as Indus International School, Greenwood High, Inventure Academy, The International School Bangalore (TISB), and Oakridge are all located within a 5-10 km radius. School buses from these institutions pick up directly from the project's security gate.

Healthcare & Social Infrastructure

Medical emergencies require immediate attention, and Mana Dale is flanked by elite healthcare facilities. Manipal Hospital, Sakra World Hospital, Motherhood, and Cloudnine are accessible within minutes. For weekend entertainment, residents can quickly reach massive retail destinations like Nexus Mall, Central Mall, and a vibrant stretch of fine-dining restaurants and microbreweries along the main road.

Elite Specifications & Construction Quality

The structural integrity and interior finishings of Mana Dale are of global standards. The developer has utilized MIVAN formwork technology for construction, ensuring a completely leak-proof, shear-wall structure that is highly resistant to seismic activity. This advanced construction method also provides a perfectly smooth finish to all walls, eliminating the need for extensive plastering.

Interior Finishings

  • Flooring: Imported large-format Italian marble dominates the living, dining, and foyer areas, exuding sheer opulence. The master bedrooms feature premium engineered wooden flooring to provide warmth and comfort. Anti-skid ceramic tiles are used in balconies and utility areas.
  • Kitchen: The kitchen is designed as a modular-ready space with multiple plug points for heavy appliances, a polished granite countertop, a double-bowl stainless steel sink, and provision for an instant geyser and water purifier.
  • Bathrooms: All bathrooms mimic a luxury spa, featuring full-height designer vitrified tiles, frameless glass shower partitions, and top-of-the-line European CP and sanitary fittings (brands like Grohe, Kohler, or Toto).

Smart Home & Security

Every home is a "Smart Home." Mana Dale integrates an advanced home automation system allowing residents to control lighting, temperature, and entertainment via a centralized iPad or their smartphones. Security is impenetrable, featuring a 5-tier protocol: biometric main door locks, video door phones, 24/7 CCTV surveillance across all common areas, RFID-controlled boom barriers for vehicles, and highly trained security personnel patrolling the perimeter.

Investment Analysis & Capital Appreciation (ROI)
