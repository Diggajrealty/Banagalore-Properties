Who We Are

Properties Bangalore is a pioneering, technology-driven real estate portal dedicated exclusively to the luxury residential market across Bangalore's most prestigious corridors. We were founded with a singular, clear mission: to bring absolute transparency, deep market intelligence, and a genuinely customer-first approach to the often opaque and complex process of buying a luxury apartment in one of India's fastest-growing cities.

We partner exclusively with Bangalore's most reputed and RERA-approved builders — including Godrej Properties, Brigade Group, Prestige Group, Sobha Limited, DSR Group, Abhee Ventures, and Mana Projects — to provide you with the most accurate, up-to-date, and legally verified project information available anywhere on the internet. Our team of certified real estate consultants possesses deep on-ground knowledge of the Sarjapur Road, Whitefield, Panathur, Varthur, and Bannerghatta Road micro-markets, ensuring that every recommendation we make is genuinely tailored to maximize your investment returns and lifestyle satisfaction.

Our Mission & Values

Transparency First

We believe every homebuyer deserves complete, unfiltered information. We publish accurate pricing, legally vetted RERA numbers, actual floor plans, and honest project timelines without any hidden agendas or paid promotions that compromise editorial integrity.

Expert Advisory

Our team of certified real estate consultants provides free, unbiased guidance tailored to your specific budget, family size, and investment goals. We help you compare projects, negotiate prices, and navigate the legal documentation process with complete confidence.

Lifetime Support

Our relationship with you doesn't end at the booking stage. We provide post-booking support including loan facilitation, legal verification, construction progress tracking, and resale assistance to ensure your property investment delivers maximum long-term value.

Why Choose Properties Bangalore?

In a market flooded with aggressive brokers and misleading advertisements, Properties Bangalore stands apart as a beacon of trusted, verified information. We are obsessive about accuracy — every project listed on our platform has been physically visited by our team, legally verified against RERA Karnataka's database, and cross-checked for price accuracy before publication. We never list projects that are not RERA-approved, and we never take commissions that compromise our editorial independence.
