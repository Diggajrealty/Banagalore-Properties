Floor Plans, Configurations & Pricing Strategy

At Brigade El Dorado, the floor plans have been exhaustively designed by international architects to cater to the diverse requirements of nuclear families, expanding households, and high-net-worth investors. Whether you are looking for a highly efficient 2 BHK or an expansive, ultra-luxury 4 BHK residence, the layouts guarantee superior space utilization.

Exquisite Layout Details

  • 2 BHK Premium Residences (1100 - 1350 Sq.ft): Designed for young professionals and small families, these units feature a massive living room, a modern open-plan kitchen, two grand bedrooms with attached bathrooms, and a wide balcony offering sunset views.
  • 3 BHK Luxury Suites (1600 - 1950 Sq.ft): The perfect blend of opulence and practicality. These units come with a dedicated dining space, an expansive utility area, three heavily ventilated bedrooms, and dual balconies that cross-ventilate the entire apartment.
  • 4 BHK Exclusive Penthouses/Villas (2400+ Sq.ft): The pinnacle of Brigade El Dorado. These ultra-spacious configurations boast private terrace gardens, separate maid's quarters with an exclusive entrance, walk-in closets, and an awe-inspiring primary suite that mirrors a 5-star hotel room.

Unrivaled 5-Star Amenities & Lifestyle

Enjoy resort-style living with multiple grand clubhouses, massive swimming pools, tennis courts, an indoor games room, and beautifully landscaped party lawns.

Location Advantage & Hyper-Connectivity

Situated in Aerospace Park, North Bangalore, the project is just 15 minutes away from the International Airport and offers seamless access to Manyata Tech Park and Shell Technology Centre.

Elite Specifications & Construction Quality

The structural integrity and interior finishings of Brigade El Dorado are of global standards. The developer has utilized MIVAN formwork technology for construction, ensuring a completely leak-proof, shear-wall structure that is highly resistant to seismic activity. This advanced construction method also provides a perfectly smooth finish to all walls, eliminating the need for extensive plastering.

Interior Finishings

  • Flooring: Imported large-format Italian marble dominates the living, dining, and foyer areas, exuding sheer opulence. The master bedrooms feature premium engineered wooden flooring to provide warmth and comfort. Anti-skid ceramic tiles are used in balconies and utility areas.
  • Kitchen: The kitchen is designed as a modular-ready space with multiple plug points for heavy appliances, a polished granite countertop, a double-bowl stainless steel sink, and provision for an instant geyser and water purifier.
  • Bathrooms: All bathrooms mimic a luxury spa, featuring full-height designer vitrified tiles, frameless glass shower partitions, and top-of-the-line European CP and sanitary fittings (brands like Grohe, Kohler, or Toto).

Smart Home & Security

Every home is a "Smart Home." Brigade El Dorado integrates an advanced home automation system allowing residents to control lighting, temperature, and entertainment via a centralized iPad or their smartphones. Security is impenetrable, featuring a 5-tier protocol: biometric main door locks, video door phones, 24/7 CCTV surveillance across all common areas, RFID-controlled boom barriers for vehicles, and highly trained security personnel patrolling the perimeter.

Investment Analysis & Capital Appreciation (ROI)

Investing in Brigade El Dorado is not merely a purchase; it is a highly strategic financial decision. The macro-economic indicators of this specific corridor point towards explosive growth over the next decade.

Historical Appreciation: Property values in this exact micro-market have seen a staggering 15% to 18% Year-on-Year appreciation over the past 4 years. Because Brigade El Dorado is currently in its early launch phase, buyers are entering the project at its lowest possible price point. By the time of physical possession, early investors typically see a minimum of 40% capital appreciation on their initial base price.

Future Infrastructure Impact: The upcoming Namma Metro Blue Line extension and the highly anticipated Peripheral Ring Road (PRR) will intersect near this project. Historically, whenever a metro line becomes fully operational, property values in a 3 km radius surge by 20-30% overnight. Furthermore, the massive influx of multinational corporations expanding their office footprints in the vicinity ensures that the rental yield for a 3 BHK in Brigade El Dorado will be highly lucrative, offering steady passive income.
