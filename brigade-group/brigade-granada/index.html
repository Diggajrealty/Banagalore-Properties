Project Overview — An Integrated Mega Township

Introducing Brigade Granada — a landmark mega-township project by the prestigious Brigade Group, strategically located on the Whitefield–Hoskote Road, Sannatammanahalli, Kadugodi, East Bangalore. This is not merely a residential project; it is an entirely self-contained urban ecosystem that redefines the concept of premium community living in one of India's fastest-growing technology corridors.

Spread across a staggering 20.19 acres of prime real estate, Brigade Granada has been masterplanned with an ambitious vision: an integrated township that seamlessly blends luxury residences with curated retail, landscaped open spaces, and world-class community infrastructure, all within a single, gated, secure environment. With 14 towering high-rise buildings, each soaring to 24 floors, the project will offer approximately 2,000 luxury apartments to discerning homebuyers and investors.

Perhaps most remarkably, Brigade Granada dedicates an extraordinary 80% of its total land area to open, green, and communal space. This industry-leading commitment to breathing room translates into lush landscaped gardens between every tower, car-free pedestrian-friendly podium levels, tree-lined promenades, and zen meditation gardens — a rarity in any Bangalore project at this scale.

These signature residences include separate servant's quarters with an independent entrance, a private family lounge adjacent to the master bedroom, extra-high ceilings, and premium floor rises that guarantee an unobstructed panoramic skyline view of East Bangalore and the surrounding Whitefield corridor.

Construction Quality & Premium Specifications

Brigade Group has consistently ranked among the top 10 developers in India in terms of construction quality and delivery credibility, with a legacy spanning over three decades and more than 275 completed projects. Brigade Granada inherits this pedigree, promising a construction quality benchmark that sets the standard for the entire Whitefield corridor.

Structural Engineering

The project employs MIVAN (Aluminium Formwork) technology — an advanced construction method used globally for high-rise buildings. MIVAN technology eliminates internal brick walls, replacing them with precision-cast monolithic RCC shear walls. The result is a structure that is significantly stronger, inherently earthquake-resistant, and provides superior acoustic and thermal insulation between apartments. The concrete mix design exceeds IS code requirements, with higher compressive strength grades deployed at all critical structural junctions. Additionally, the entire structure is designed to comply with IS 1893 seismic zone specifications for Bangalore's earthquake risk classification.
